ABR → Infinite Painter brush converter

Turns Photoshop brushes (.abr) into a pack (.przp) you can import into Infinite Painter. Everything runs in your browser: your brushes are never uploaded.

    Reads Photoshop 7 and newer brushes (ABR v6–v10). A partial brush still converts, but loses a feature Infinite Painter does not have.
